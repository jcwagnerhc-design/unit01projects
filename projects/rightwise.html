LEARN WITH CONTEXT

Understand the reason. Know the limits.

Start with U.S. foundations, then explore the available state lessons. Each lesson teaches an idea, explains an example, and asks you to apply it. Correct answers unlock the next section; they do not guarantee that you can apply the law in every situation.

Make the learning your own

After a lesson, explain its principle without looking at the answers. Change one fact in the example and consider whether your reasoning changes. Name a detail you would need to investigate before drawing a conclusion.

Use the lesson’s “Source and limits” links to compare the explanation with the underlying source. If something is unclear, revisit the explanation before continuing.

Coverage, sources, and progress

This is an introductory course, not a complete guide to U.S. law. State paths currently add recording-law lessons for California, New York, and Texas. Choosing a state does not make every lesson a complete statement of that state’s law.

Each lesson links to its sources and explains limits. Laws and interpretations can change; a source-check date is not a promise that the material remains current. The course is general education, not advice about your individual situation.

Completed lessons and XP are saved in this browser on this device, not synced between devices. Clearing browser data removes them. On a shared device, learners share this browser’s progress. A lesson in progress can be resumed during the current page session. Progress options let you reset completions and undo the latest reset.

Human decisions and AI contributions

The student creator set the purpose, requested U.S. and state coverage, supplied the visual direction, and asked for a progressive learning map. After finding the experience too focused on testing, the creator requested deeper teaching, broader questions, and a required check in every section.

OpenAI’s Codex drafted the lesson explanations, examples, questions, and code, and assisted with testing and publishing. These contributions were revised in response to the creator’s feedback. AI assistance does not make the content authoritative or establish the creator’s personal legal expertise.

The creator chose to finish without the proposed conversational AI tutor. There is no live AI tutor in this version. The learning-path design was inspired by Duolingo; Rightwise is not affiliated with Duolingo.
