💾 Ta progression est enregistrée dans ce navigateur, sur cet ordinateur. (Your progress is saved in this browser, on this computer.)

Comment jouer (how to play)

🥐1 · Le Petit-Déjeuner

Read the French word, click the picture.

🍓2 · Le Goûter

Click the picture, then choose le, la, l’ or les.

🍝3 · Le Déjeuner

See the picture, type the French word.

🍽️4 · Le Dîner

Cook a whole menu: l'entrée, le plat principal, le dessert.

🍷5 · Le Restaurant

Meals, the menu and verbs: type them all!

🎮Les jeux

Finish Level 1 to open Le Café, La Pluie de Nourriture and Le Labyrinthe du Chef.

⭐Les étoiles

Up to 5 stars per recipe, the first time you finish a level. Completing a mini-game gives 1 star. Spend them to dress up the chef!

À propos de ce jeu (about this game)

Dillon Allen is responsible for everything this game has to offer: its design, its French, and everything it teaches. Dillon Allen created it with the help of Claude, a service of Anthropic.

The French vocabulary was supplied by Dillon Allen and comes from Lawless French: https://www.lawlessfrench.com/vocabulary/food/
