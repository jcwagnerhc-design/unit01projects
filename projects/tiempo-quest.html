A small change, a big clue!

Stem changes!

The stem is what remains when you remove -ar, -er, or -ir: pensar → pens-. Some verbs change a vowel inside that stem.

1. Present: the “boot” pattern!

The vowel changes for yo, tú, él/ella/usted, and ellos/ellas/ustedes. Nosotros and vosotros keep the original stem!

e → ie
pensar → piensoI think · pensamos = we thinko → ue
poder → puedoI can · podemos = we cane → i
pedir → pidoI ask for · pedimos = we ask foru → ue
jugar → juegoI play · jugamos = we play
See the whole present-tense pattern

yo pienso · tú piensas · ella piensa
nosotros pensamos · vosotros pensáis · ellos piensan

Only some verbs have stem changes. Learn which pattern goes with each verb!

2. Preterite: a different rule!

For these -ir verbs, change the stem only for él/ella/usted and ellos/ellas/ustedes.

o → u: dormir → durmió / durmieron
She slept / they slept.

e → i: pedir → pidió / pidieron
She asked for / they asked for.

Also: repetir → repitió / repitieron.

Compare: yo dormí, but ella durmió!

Don’t carry the present “boot” into the past: ella duerme = she sleeps; ella durmió = she slept.

About Tiempo Quest

AI Use and Diligence Statement

By Anya Donohue

I, Anya Donohue, used ChatGPT (Codex) to help develop Tiempo Quest. AI assisted with brainstorming, writing instructions, coding, and creating game content. My own contributions included designing the game, selecting the Spanish vocabulary and verb tenses, developing the correctness scale, and writing and revising instructions.

To check the AI-assisted work, I tested the game myself and gathered testing and feedback from my peers and Mr. Devaney. I reviewed the Spanish content using class materials I provided, my own knowledge, and my teacher’s knowledge. Based on this feedback, I revised the game’s flow and instructions to help players understand its purpose and how to play.

The game’s feedback on written answers uses rule-based JavaScript checks developed with AI assistance, rather than a live AI model during gameplay. This feedback may contain mistakes.
